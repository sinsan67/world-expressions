About this project

A love letter
to idioms.

Every language has a few expressions that refuse to translate. They carry too much — too much history, too much local colour, too much of the way people actually speak. That's exactly where World Expressions starts.

"Casser les pieds" literally means to break someone's feet. But in French, it means to bore them to death. Which makes you wonder what feet have to do with boredom.

This project collects those moments — in French, English, Spanish, Italian, Turkish — and makes them searchable, comparable, explorable. It's free, open source, and built by someone who finds this kind of thing genuinely interesting.

The database holds over 1 500 expressions, growing. Meanings, origins, usage examples, and cross-language equivalents when they exist.

About

A project about language, culture, and the small things that make each one unique.

🌍
What is World Expressions?
A free, open exploration of idiomatic expressions from around the world. Type a word — discover how different languages say the same thing in wildly different ways.
💬
Why expressions?
Literal translation is never enough. Expressions carry history, humour, and the texture of daily life. A Turkish proverb about wolves, a French saying about feet — each one is a window into a culture.
📚
The data
Over 1 500 expressions across 5 languages (French, English, Spanish, Italian, Turkish). Meaning, origin, usage example, and cross-language equivalents.
🔓
Open source
Built in public. Contributions — expressions, corrections, ideas — are welcome.
