## 1. avoir-un-poil-dans-la-main.png

🇫🇷 "Avoir un poil dans la main" — literally, to have a hair growing in your hand. ✋

It means to be bone idle: so allergic to work that hair could sprout on your palm.

We all know someone. (Tagging them is effort too, though.) 😏

## 2. donner-sa-langue-au-chat.png

🇫🇷 "Donner sa langue au chat" — literally, to give your tongue to the cat. 🐱

It means to give up guessing: you surrender — just tell me the answer.

Fun fact: the older version gave your tongue "to the dogs." The cat stole the phrase in the 1700s.

## 3. quand-les-poules-auront-des-dents.png

🇫🇷 "Quand les poules auront des dents" — literally, when hens have teeth. 🐔

Meaning: never. On a day that will simply never come.

English says "when pigs fly." French bet on toothless chickens. Which is weirder?

## 4. les-carottes-sont-cuites.png

🇫🇷 "Les carottes sont cuites" — literally, the carrots are cooked. 🥕

Meaning: it's game over — the situation is lost, nothing left to do.

Because a cooked carrot never goes back to raw. What's done is done.
